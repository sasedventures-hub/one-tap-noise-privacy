Privacy Policy

One Tap Noise · Last updated 3 November 2026

Short version: One Tap Noise does not collect or share any personal data. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. All sounds are generated live on your device; the app does not use the internet to play them.

What stays on your device

A few app preferences are saved on your device so the app works the way you left it:

  • the last sound you played and your tone setting
  • whether you've unlocked Pro
  • whether you've seen the one-time "swipe for more sounds" hint
  • how many times you've opened the app, used only to decide when to show Apple's standard rating prompt

None of this is personal information, and it is never sent to us. Like any app's settings, it may be included in your own iCloud or device backup, and it is removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

The optional one-time Pro unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, name or Apple Account. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Siri, widgets and Control Center

These features run on your device through Apple's frameworks. The app receives only the action you requested (for example "start brown noise") and nothing else.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if analytics were added), this page will be updated and the App Store privacy label changed to match before the update ships.
