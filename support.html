Email sasedventures@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open the app, tap the gear icon (top left), then Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro. Pro is a one-time purchase — there is no subscription.

How do I start a sound without opening the app?

  • Control Center (iOS 18 or later): open Control Center, long-press an empty area, tap Add a Control and search for Noise. It plays your last-used sound.
  • Widgets: long-press your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget and choose One Tap Noise. Long-press the widget and tap Edit Widget to pick its sound. Stack several widgets on top of each other to swipe between sounds.
  • Siri: say "Start brown noise in One Tap Noise" or "Stop noise in One Tap Noise".

Why did the sound stop?

  • Your headphones or AirPods disconnected — the app stops so sound doesn't suddenly play from the speaker.
  • A phone call, alarm or Siri interrupted it. The sound resumes afterwards when iOS allows it.
  • A sleep timer finished. Timers fade out gently over the last 30 seconds.

Does it keep playing when my phone is locked?

Yes. Sound keeps playing with the screen locked and with the silent switch on. You can pause it from the Lock Screen or with your AirPods.

What's free and what's Pro?

Free forever: brown and white noise, background playback, Lock Screen controls, Control Center and Siri.
Pro (one-time): pink, green and blue noise, the sleep timer, crossfade when you swipe, widgets for every sound and the tone control. Tap any Pro sound to hear a free 15-second preview first.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.
